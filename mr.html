Your sketchbook, standing in the room.

Begin by lifting a difficulty seal onto the page. Grab troops by a raised arm and drop them across the green rally area to choose their route. Cannons sit on raised docks beside your left base. One dock is ready; build up to four on the dashed outlines. Held pieces highlight their landing spot. Drop the eraser on any of your cannons to sell it and keep its dock. Toss potions onto the page.

Guide your army: pinch a deployed troop, move a little sideways and release to suggest a route. Drop the suggestion near an enemy cannon or base to mark that objective. Troops walk there; nearby fighting can take priority. Both armies can destroy cannons, leaving their docks ready for replacements.

Quest 3: pinch with your fingers, or use controller grips. Point and squeeze the trigger to reach distant pieces. Carry a drawn ring; hold two rings to resize and turn.

Preview: drag pieces. Right-drag to orbit, scroll to zoom; two fingers orbit and zoom on touchscreens.

The hourglass pauses; the clock changes speed. The music box toggles the original soundtrack. Lift the feather to change mist and detail. Pause before using the new-page sketch. The compass leaves MR.
